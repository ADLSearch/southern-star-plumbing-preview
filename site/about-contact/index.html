About Southern Star Plumbing

A Florida plumbing contractor with roots in Escambia County and a history of plumbing work connected to residential and building projects.

Southern Star Plumbing, Inc. is based in Molino, Florida. The current website states the company was established in 2003. The Florida plumbing license RF11067137 was issued 16 September 2003 and, as of a 23 November 2026 check of the Florida DBPR record, is listed as Florida Registered Plumbing Contractor, status Current, Active, expiring 31 August 2027.

Our work centers on the fundamentals: clear project requirements, dependable communication, and attention to the plumbing systems that keep properties operating properly. We work with property owners, builders, and project teams on defined scopes of plumbing work.

Whether a job involves new installation, improvements to an existing property, or construction-related plumbing, we welcome the opportunity to discuss the requirements and determine the appropriate next steps.

Nationwide inquiries
