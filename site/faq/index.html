FAQ

Questions about Southern Star Plumbing

Answers below stay with facts the company has published or that appear on the Florida license record. If something is not listed — hours, prices, warranties — we say so.

Where do you work?

Our home base is Molino, Florida, and we serve Northwest Florida and the surrounding region.

Do you take projects outside Florida?

We welcome inquiries from across the United States. Work outside our locally licensed area is reviewed case by case and done only where Southern Star Plumbing, Inc. or appropriately licensed partners are authorized to perform it.

Who do you work with?

Homeowners, general contractors, construction teams, property owners, and businesses looking for plumbing-related project support.

What should I include when I contact you?

The project location (city and state), the type of work, and your desired start date and completion schedule. That lets us quickly review whether we can help.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Southern Star Plumbing, Inc. is a Florida Registered Plumbing Contractor, license RF11067137. Confirm the current status with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

What plumbing services do you offer?

The company lists new construction plumbing, water heater work, fixtures and piping, gas piping projects where permitted, renovations and improvements, and contractor coordination. Contact us to confirm availability and scope for your specific job.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

The current website does not advertise 24/7 or emergency dispatch. For project inquiries, call (863) 222-0924 or send the details through the quote form. If a situation is urgent, call and say so when you reach the office.

How do I request a quote?

Call (863) 222-0924, email southernstarplumbing@outlook.com, or use the “Discuss a Project” form with your name, phone, location, type of work, timeline, and project details. We’ll review whether we can help.

Where is the office?

Southern Star Plumbing, Inc. is based in Molino, Florida 32577. The Florida license and public directories list 3655 Crabtree Church Rd, Molino, FL 32577.

When was the company established?

The current website states the company was established in 2003. The Florida plumbing license was issued 16 September 2003.

Who is the qualifying contractor?

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation license lists Rick Gene Davidson as the primary name for Southern Star Plumbing Inc.

Do you publish prices online?

No price list is published on the current website. Project work is reviewed from the scope you send. Call or request a quote and we will review whether we can help.

Is gas piping always included?

No. Gas piping work is offered where permitted under the applicable licensing and project requirements. We’ll review that when we look at your scope.

What is a Registered Plumbing Contractor in Florida?

It is the license type printed on Southern Star’s site and on the Florida DBPR record (license RF11067137). A Florida registration does not itself authorize plumbing work in every jurisdiction or state. We review authorization for the job location before taking work on.

What are your hours?

Hours are not listed on the current website, and public directories do not all agree. Please call (863) 222-0924 to confirm availability.
